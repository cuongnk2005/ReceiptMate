CONCEPT A Linear Precision: Dark Monospace & High-Density Technical

Concept A: Design Review

Design Rationale

Inspired by modern engineering tools (Linear, Raycast). Utilizes a deep zinc-slate palette (`#090D16`), sharp 1px borders, and monospace meta-labels (`JetBrains Mono`). Focuses on maximum information density, low optical glare, and keyboard-centric velocity.

Interaction Risks

1. Low contrast on low-grade mobile displays if ambient sunlight is high.
2. Power-user dark styling may feel intimidating to non-technical collaborators (marketing/content).

Review Notes

- Preserves 100% of the single-interaction contract: 4 fixed statuses, modal validation, drag/touch/keyboard movement, rollback retry.
- Notice the Segmented Tab switcher on screen widths < 768px.
