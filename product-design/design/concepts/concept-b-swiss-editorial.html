Concept B Swiss Editorial: Warm gallery paper, serif headlines & architectural hairlines

Concept B Review

Design Philosophy

Treated like a printed architectural monograph. Embraces warm ivory backgrounds (`#F6F5F0`), deep ink blacks, and serif display titles (`Newsreader`). Minimizes shadow-heavy GUI tropes in favor of spatial tension and clean hairlines.

Interaction Risks

1. Generous margins and font sizes consume more vertical screen space than compact terminal tables.
2. Users accustomed to dark mode may experience glare in dark room settings.

Contract Fidelity

Preserves 100% identical functional parity: 4 statuses, modal validations, drag-and-drop, mobile touch move menu, and simulated rollback on error.
