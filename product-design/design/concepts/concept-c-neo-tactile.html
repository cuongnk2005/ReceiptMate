CONCEPT C Neo-Tactile: Soft Dimensionality, Claymorphism & Rounded Pill Surfaces

Concept C Review

Design Philosophy

Tactile friendly productivity (inspired by Craft Docs and modern iOS). Features soft dual-layer shadows, pill badges, bouncy tap scale feedback, and generous 16px rounded curves. Reduces intimidation for cross-functional teams.

Interaction Risks

1. Higher visual weight per card due to soft drop shadows.
2. Requires precise color balancing to ensure pill badges remain readable against card white.

Contract Parity

Fully implements the exact same 4-status model, modal checks, drag-and-drop, touch move popovers, and network rollback simulations.
